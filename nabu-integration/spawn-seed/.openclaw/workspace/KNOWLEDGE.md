## 6. NABU Credits System

NABU operates on a unified credit system. Every AI interaction consumes credits from a single monthly pool.

**Credit Weights:**

- Assists (email drafts, Q&A, summaries, translation): 1 credit each
- Automations (auto-send email, create task, schedule meeting): 5 credits each

**Research reports** are tracked separately from credits — allocated as monthly packs per tier.

**Credit Top-Ups:**

- 50 credits: $10
- 125 credits: $17.50 (12% discount)
- 250 credits: $30 (20% discount)
- Available on all paid tiers. Not available on Free.
- Top-up credits expire at end of calendar month (no rollover).

**Economy Mode:**

- At 85% credit usage, users are offered the choice to switch to Economy Mode.
- Economy Mode uses lighter AI models to stretch remaining credits further.
- Routine tasks (email drafts, task creation, scheduling) perform comparably in Economy Mode.
- Users can also choose to purchase a top-up instead.

## 7. Onboarding Process (Client)

1. **Sign up** — Create account via email + password, verify email.
2. **Welcome** — Receive welcome communication and introduction to the platform.
3. **Questionnaire** — Share business needs, role requirements, and preferences.
4. **VA Matching** — VA.Team introduces 2-3 vetted VA candidates within 72 hours of signing.
5. **Selection** — Client reviews profiles and selects their VA.
6. **Setup** — Prepare task list, access credentials, and communication rhythm.
7. **Go Live** — VA begins work. Client communicates directly with VA using preferred tools.

**Recommended preparation before VA starts:**

- A clear task list and role expectations
- Access credentials for necessary software and files
- A communication rhythm (daily or weekly check-ins)

## 10. Data Security & Privacy (Client-Facing Summary)

- All VAs sign confidentiality agreements (NDAs) before placement.
- VA.Team enforces strict compliance with data protection policies.
- Content processed by NABU AI is not used for training or improving AI models.
- NABU AI interactions remain confidential and subject to the same privacy protections as all platform data.
- VA.Team does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.
- VA.Team does not use data for targeted advertising.
- Payment processing is handled by PCI DSS-compliant processors (Stripe).
- The platform is not designed for Regulated Data (PHI, PCI raw data, FINRA/SEC data, FERPA, COPPA, ITAR/EAR). Users must not upload such data.
- Data breach notification within 72 hours where required by law.
- GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and multi-state U.S. privacy law compliance.

**For full details:** Refer users to the Privacy Policy at www.va.team and Terms & Conditions.
