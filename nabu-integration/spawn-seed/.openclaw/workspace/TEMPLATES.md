# TEMPLATES — NABU AI Response & Document Templates

Standard templates NABU uses when generating common outputs. These ensure consistency with VA.Team's brand voice and professional standards.

---

## 1. Email Templates

### 1.1 Professional Email (Default Structure)

```
Subject: [Clear, specific subject line — no clickbait]

Hi [First Name],

[Opening — 1 sentence establishing context or purpose]

[Body — 2-4 sentences with the key information, request, or update]

[Closing — 1 sentence with clear next step or call to action]

Best regards,
[Sender Name]
[Title] | [Company]
```

### 1.2 Follow-Up Email

```
Subject: Following up — [Original Topic]

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to follow up on [specific topic/previous conversation]. [1 sentence reminder of context.]

[Body — what you need or what has changed since last contact]

Would [specific next step] work for you? Happy to adjust based on your availability.

Best,
[Sender Name]
```

### 1.3 Meeting Request

```
Subject: Meeting Request — [Topic]

Hi [First Name],

I'd like to schedule [duration] to discuss [specific topic]. Would any of the following work?

- [Option 1: Day, Date, Time, Timezone]
- [Option 2: Day, Date, Time, Timezone]
- [Option 3: Day, Date, Time, Timezone]

If none of these work, please suggest a time that suits you and I'll make it happen.

Best,
[Sender Name]
```

## 2. Task Brief Template

When helping users create task briefs for their VA:

```
## Task: [Clear Task Title]

**Objective:** [1-2 sentences — what needs to be accomplished]

**Deliverables:**
- [Specific deliverable 1]
- [Specific deliverable 2]

**Deadline:** [Date and time with timezone]

**Context:** [Background information the VA needs to do this well]

**Tools/Access Needed:** [Software, accounts, files required]

**Success Criteria:** [How to know this is done correctly]

**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low]

**Notes:** [Any additional instructions or preferences]
```

## 6. Standard Disclaimer Blocks

### Professional Advice Disclaimer

> "This information is for general reference only and does not constitute professional [legal/medical/financial/tax] advice. Please consult a qualified professional for guidance specific to your situation."

### Data Accuracy Disclaimer

> "This data was compiled from available sources as of [date]. Market conditions and figures may have changed. Please verify critical data points before making business decisions."
